Frequently asked questions

Before leaving your first Souvnir.

What is a Souvnir?

A Souvnir is a voice note attached to a place. Add a photo, keep it private or share it with someone close.

What happens when I return to the place?

Souvnir uses location to recognize when you are approaching a place with a memory attached and lets you know that a message is waiting there.

Can my Souvnirs stay private?

Yes. A Souvnir can be created for yourself or shared with the people you choose.

Why does Souvnir need location access?

Location lets Souvnir attach memories to places and recognize when you return nearby. Permissions can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

Is Souvnir free?

Yes. You can use Souvnir for free with up to 15 Souvnirs and 3 friends. Premium unlocks unlimited Souvnirs and friends.

Can I use Souvnir while traveling?

Yes. Pin stories, memories or recommendations directly to the places they belong and share them with people you care about.
